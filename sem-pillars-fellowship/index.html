CONFIRMATION OF SUPPORT  ·  BASEM TRAVELLING FELLOWSHIP 2026

Summary

My practice is strongest where orthopaedic surgery and elite sport meet, and thinner elsewhere. Very few clinicians practise across all three domains, and no single centre teaches all three well, which is why this is proposed as a series of short attachments rather than one long one. Each is paired with work already under way, and the fellowship would facilitate collaborations that are multidisciplinary, multifocal and multicentre.

The timing is relevant, since BASEM and the Faculty of Sport & Exercise Medicine are merging to form a new College of Sport, Exercise & Musculoskeletal Medicine. I intend to build this fellowship around developing the knowledge, skills and experience required to make a valued and sustained contribution to the College, and to help drive the specialty into an era of greater impact as an independent, yet increasingly integrated, clinical and academic field of practice.

01

Team and athlete care

Athlete health systems, injury and illness prevention, and the safe delivery of sporting events.

02

Musculoskeletal medicine

Sports injury and trauma across the life course, including children, young people and people with disability.

03

Exercise medicine

Physical activity, obesity and cardiometabolic health, joint preservation, and population services.

Outline

Dublin
Clinical and academic practice across general and performance populations; integrated surgical, medical and specialist rehabilitation care of upper and lower limb injury in elite sport; the training, governance and service development structures of a separately constituted Faculty. In 2027 Dublin hosts the 10th UEFA Medical Symposium, and the Irish Open in nearby Kildare.
Scotland
Sports event medicine at championship and community level; injury and illness surveillance at an IOC research centre; national clinical pathway design, and the governance and health economics of elective services; sports knee surgery, robot-assisted surgery, and sports traumatology including state of the art care in muscle and tendon injuries.
London
Leadership and education in elite sports medicine and surgery; sports knee surgery from elite performance to healthy active living; event medicine epidemiology and the older athlete; physical activity engagement through digital and social media, and cross-sector collaboration between health and social care, business and sport.
Berlin
Rehabilitation, strength and conditioning, and physical activity engagement delivered outside a health service.

Afterwards

  • Applied first in my own clinics and in the sporting environments I cover.
  • A minimum dataset for event care surveillance, and completion of the Sports Medicine Event Care framework.
